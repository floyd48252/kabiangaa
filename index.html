KABIANGA HIGH SCHOOL

Our Motto: HARD WORK AND DISCIPLINE FOR SUCCESS

One of Kenya's most prestigious national schools with a century-long reputation for academic excellence.

Situated amidst the lush, serene emerald tea estates of Belgut Sub-County in Kericho County, Kabianga High School stands as a beacon of transformative boys' secondary education in Kenya. Founded in 1925 as a government African school and elevated to National School status, Kabianga has consistently shaped generations of principled scholars, innovators, and national leaders.

Our academic programs seamlessly integrate rigorous Senior School CBE pathways—spanning STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics), Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports Science—supported by ultra-modern science laboratories, digital ICT hubs, a well-stocked resource library, and a highly dedicated faculty committed to individual student mentorship.

Beyond classroom instruction, life at "The School on the Hill" fosters all-round personal growth. Students actively participate in KSSSA championship athletics, rugby, football, basketball, Kenya Science and Engineering Fair (KSEF), national drama and music festivals, and vibrant peer-led clubs.

Kabianga

Embodiment of Discipline & Excellence

Since its establishment in 1925, Kabianga High School has stood on the principle that true education harmonizes intellectual rigor with unwavering moral character. Every student who walks through our gates is challenged to pursue personal mastery, respect diversity, and uphold self-discipline in both thought and action.

Our proud alumni network spans medicine, engineering, judiciary, academia, public governance, and international athletics—testament to an enduring school culture where mentorship, spirituality, and hard work transform young boys into compassionate, visionary citizens.
